1 The Problem

High-Strength Sludge & Regulatory Blindspots

Who Faces It

• Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) officers and ETP plant operators.
• Chemical synthesis, bulk drug (API), and textile manufacturing units.
• Common Effluent Treatment Plant (CETP) operating consortiums.

The Failure Today

• Chemical plants generate hazardous coagulation sludge with high organic COD (>100,000 mg/L) and acidic pH (5.5).
• Unmonitored storage risks heavy metal leaching (Cr, Ni, Pb, Cd) and septic H₂S emissions.
• Transport without Form 10 manifests invites immediate statutory notices.

How Big It Is

• 8.5+ Million Tonnes of hazardous sludge produced in India annually (CPCB).
• ₹10L - ₹50L in environmental compensation and closure orders.
• Section 15 Environment Protection Act 1986 imposes up to 5 years imprisonment or ₹1L fine.

Why Today's Way Fails

• Paper Logbooks: Daily shift entries are disconnected from legal retention limits.
• Static NABL Certs: Lab reports sit in filing cabinets without driving actual press recipes.
• Zero Early Warning: Plants realize they exceeded the 90-day storage cap only after SPCB inspections.

2 Our Solution

Zero-Assumption Telemetry + RAG + 3D Twin

ETP SludgeOps helps industrial ETP operators and EHS compliance managers to track, dewater, and audit hazardous chemical sludge by combining a zero-assumption telemetry engine, Groq-powered statutory RAG knowledge base, and an interactive 3D digital twin.

Benefit 1

Automated 90-Day Legal Audit

• Continuous countdown toward MoEFCC HOWM Rule 8 storage limit (90 days).
• Real-time retention cap tracking against 150T KSPCB consent.
• 1-click Form 10 highway manifest generation.
• Zero Section 15 EPA violations.

Benefit 2

Lab-to-Press Optimization

• Translates certified NABL lab reports into exact dewatering recipes.
• Prescribes lime neutralization (pH 5.5) + cationic polyacrylamide.
• Optimizes plate & frame press cycle (16 bar, 2.5 hr).
• Achieves >70% volume reduction.

Benefit 3

Grounded Regulatory RAG

• Groq LLM (openai/gpt-oss-120b) + BM25 statutory vector retriever.
• Queries official gazettes: HOWM 2016, KSPCB CTO 782, CPCB AFR.
• Exact citations & legal penalty notices with 98% confidence.
• Strict zero-assumption policy.

5 User Workflow

Step-by-Step Operator Journey
STEP 1

Daily Shift Entry

Operator logs daily wastewater flow (m³/d), raw COD load, coagulant doses, and wet sludge mass.

STEP 2

Lab Characterisation

Chemist records certified NABL assay: heavy metals (Cr, Ni, Pb, Cd), reactive sulphides, & TCLP leachability.

STEP 3

Dewatering Recipe

Plant supervisor applies the AI Simplest Solution recipe (lime + polymer) to the plate & frame press.

STEP 4

Covered Shed Storage

System tracks cumulative mass against the 150T CTO cap and triggers automated countdown before 90-day expiry.

STEP 5

TSDF Manifest

Generates Form 10 manifests, validates GPS carrier registration, and logs KWMP Dabaspet gate receipt.

6 Data Flow and Architecture

End-to-End System Integration

Input Data Streams

• Daily Shift Telemetry: Wastewater flow m³/d, Influent COD (>100,000 mg/L), pH (5.5).
• NABL Lab Reports: Heavy metals, reactive sulphides, capillary suction time (CST).
• Regulatory PDFs: MoEFCC HOWM Rules 2016, KSPCB Consent No. 782, CPCB AFR.

AI & Processing Layer

• Deterministic Telemetry: COD mass balance, dry solids, volume reduction equations.
• BM25 Statutory Vector Index: 20+ gazette provisions with section-level metadata.
• Groq LLM (openai/gpt-oss-120b): Fast context-augmented generation.
• SHA-256 Audit Hasher: Cryptographic evidence proof.

Output Deliverables

• Real-Time SaaS Dashboards: Status banners, KPIs, and disposal deadline alerts.
• 3D WebGL Digital Twin: Spatial equipment representation.
• 3-Step Dewatering Prescriptions: Chemical conditioning recipes.
• Form 10 Manifests: Verified 7-copy highway transport documentation.

7 How We Use AI

Model or Tool What It Does in Our Product Why We Chose It

openai/gpt-oss-120b		 Synthesizes contextual regulatory answers and interprets complex physicochemical lab assays. Sub-second inference speed, exceptional reasoning over statutory legal texts, zero GPU idle cost.

BM25 Retriever		 Indexes official gazettes (HOWM 2016, KSPCB CTO) into semantic passages with section metadata. Deterministic zero-hallucination grounding; runs embedded in Node.js backend with 0ms cloud latency.
Deterministic
Compliance Engine		 Evaluates sludge storage age, shed capacity caps, and statutory violation thresholds. 100% auditable; law cannot be left to probabilistic guessing; guarantees exact EPA Section 15 notices.

AI Guardrails & Zero-Assumption Policy:

• What AI is NOT allowed to do: The AI is strictly prohibited from inventing missing sludge measurements (zero-assumption data policy) or fabricating legal limits outside approved official gazettes.
• What happens when unsure: If retrieved passages do not contain an exact statutory provision, the system explicitly states: "No approved regulatory provision retrieved — consult SPCB environmental officer", completely eliminating speculative legal advice.
